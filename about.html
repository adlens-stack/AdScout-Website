01 / What we do

One dashboard for every dollar your competitors spend.

We continuously index Meta and Google ad activity across your category, normalise it into spend, share-of-voice, and creative patterns, and put it in front of the people who decide where the budget goes.

No screenshots. No spreadsheets. No three-week agency turnaround.

02 / Why we built it

Because "our competitor is doing something" isn't a strategy.

Our team came out of CEE retail and pharma — categories where one competitor moving budget can swing a quarter. We watched senior marketers make €1M+ decisions on partial data, then defend them in board rooms with the same partial data.

AdScout is the tool we wanted on the other side of those meetings.

03 / What we believe
PRINCIPLE 01 Show the number, not the vibe. Every chart traces back to a raw observation. If we can't source it, we don't show it.
PRINCIPLE 02 CMOs read in minutes, not hours. The dashboard is built for a 7-minute Monday review. Depth is one click away — never in your face.
PRINCIPLE 03 No vanity metrics. If a number doesn't change a budget decision, it doesn't belong on the screen.
PRINCIPLE 04 Privacy is non-negotiable. EU-hosted, GDPR-first, public-data-only. No cookies, fingerprints, or PII — ever.

01 / Čo robíme

Jeden dashboard pre každé euro, ktoré minie vaša konkurencia.

Priebežne indexujeme reklamnú aktivitu na Meta a Google v rámci vašej kategórie, normalizujeme ju do spendu, share-of-voice a kreatívnych vzorov, a dávame ju ľuďom, ktorí rozhodujú o budgete.

Žiadne screenshoty. Žiadne spreadsheety. Žiadny trojtýždňový agentúrny turnaround.

02 / Prečo sme to postavili

Pretože „konkurencia niečo robí" nie je stratégia.

Náš tím prišiel z CEE retailu a pharmy — kategórií, kde jeden konkurent presúvajúci budget vie obrátiť kvartál. Sledovali sme, ako seniorní marketéri robia rozhodnutia za €1M+ na neúplných dátach — a potom ich obhajujú v boardroome s tými istými neúplnými dátami.

AdScout je nástroj, ktorý sme chceli mať na druhej strane stola.
